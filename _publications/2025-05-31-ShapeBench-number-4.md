Aerodynamic shape optimization remains difficult to benchmark and compare across methods because datasets, evaluation protocols, and quality metrics vary widely across the literature. ShapeBench addresses this gap by introducing a large-scale benchmark and diagnostic suite for aerodynamic shape optimization. The benchmark includes standardized datasets, physics-informed evaluation metrics, and a framework for comparing optimization outcomes across representative aerodynamic design problems. We highlight how ShapeBench enables more consistent performance analysis and better diagnosis of optimization behavior across architectures and problem settings.
