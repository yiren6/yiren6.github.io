This dissertation develops multi-fidelity aerodynamic surrogate modeling frameworks for conceptual design using graph neural networks. It introduces methods for augmenting low-fidelity aerodynamic tools with high-fidelity CFD field information, builds and validates graph-based prediction models for delta-wing aerodynamics, and demonstrates their use in design-space exploration and optimization under realistic aerodynamic constraints.
